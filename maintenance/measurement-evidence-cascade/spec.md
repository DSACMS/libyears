## Human authority and minimum claim (C1)

The commissioning human selects a progressive evidence cascade and authorizes
specification, independent reviews, planning, sequential tasks, implementation,
verification, read-only OASim smoke and local commits. No push. Use the strongest
available accepted evidence, requiring only evidence necessary for the actual
claim. Missing stronger assurance must narrow the claim, never silently weaken it.

The measurement claim is: accepted evidence establishes attributable direct
occurrence P at selected/used version V, with public registry R the applicable
publication history for version age. The minimum floor requires supported direct
population, exact attributable selection, applicable public namespace BEFORE
lookup, authoritative selected/latest identities and dates, existing arithmetic
and honest known/unknown/unavailable coverage, and inspectable limitations.
Unknown/private/conflicting namespace never falls back to a public namesake.

Acquisition (exact bytes historically obtained from R), content correspondence
(local bytes matching public artifact), reproducibility (repeatable historical
resolution/build) and deployment/runtime (executed version) are separate claims.
No number implies any of them. Accepted testimony is testimony, not authenticated
history. Hash agreement is a check, not proof of acquisition or reproducibility.

Their existing mechanics, scopes, date/comparator policies and output remain

not be represented as satisfying a new positive namespace gate. Do not copy that

## Lock and population determination (C3)

Choose A, declaration-first coherence, for this migration. Retain complete supported
project parsing, sole framework/context, unsupported MSBuild influence protections,
request-minimum coherence, exact byte binding, direct identity association and
source-qualified occurrence weighting. Unsupported project population remains
unknown; missing whole lock/witness retains independently known N with null
measurement/coverage; accepted whole evidence permits individual failures/M/N.
Successful siblings survive. No parser expansion or guessed N=51.

An accepted schema1 lock records Direct IDs, resolved versions, requested ranges,
and a framework key; closure/Project entries do not count. It contains no project
identity, freshness proof, imported-input completeness, applicable registry,
actual acquisition event, local artifact proof, deployment or reproducibility
certificate. Co-location, Git presence, mtime or ID/version overlap are insufficient.
Minimum acceptance retains supported project/context, whole-lock framing, matching
framework, no extra Direct IDs, per-declaration request coherence and explicit
byte/context/completeness/namespace testimony. A later testimony is admissible
for this snapshot claim, without claiming contemporaneity.

Alternative B, resolved-snapshot population, could truthfully describe a sufficiently
bound lock's historical Direct set, but would change the directness/completeness
convention and require separately accepted context/population testimony when
project constructs cannot be interpreted. It cannot establish current/evaluated
project completeness from the lock alone. Conditions/multi-targeting/RID/imports
may change the graph; a single lock framework cannot establish omitted contexts.
A rejects unsupported constructs and remains easy to audit, at the cost of yield;
B improves retained-snapshot usability only under that different bounded claim.
Both can avoid execution, but A's extension toward wider syntax risks recreating
MSBuild, while B must not pretend to reproduce it. A requires no new resolver or
population contract and is the smallest truthful migration. Preserve B as future
work, not a silent fallback after parsing or row failures.
