## Human decision

The binding invariant is:

> DECLARED AND OBSERVED ARE TWO TRUTHFUL VIEWS OF DEPENDENCY AGE.

Declared answers what version the repository requests. Observed answers what
accepted retained dependency-system evidence establishes. Neither implies runtime,
deployment, installed state, telemetry or a new resolution. Neither replaces the
other. Facts may share an occurrence only with supported association. Availability,
population, coverage and statistics remain separate. Never add the two age totals,
force symmetric availability, invent a gap metric or alter365.25-day arithmetic.

module weighting, rejects arbitrary name/purpose filters, permits ordinary project
and plugin populations to remain distinct, and authorizes a bounded static Maven
subset. Initial Maven observed results may be unavailable. Gradle implementation,
new supplier-evidence formats and sibling pluralization are not required.

not unreviewed reuse of NuGet source policy. Future authorized public proving-ground
